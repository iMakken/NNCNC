The Developer

Designed for
absolute focus.

As a developer and cyclist, I built NNCNC to eliminate the digital noise of modern telemetry. Inspired by hardware computers and high-performance engineering, this tracker brings core metrics to the front with uncompromising speed and visual clarity.

The Core

Your bike.
Not just your ride.

NNCNC tracks the bike as well as the rider. An odometer counts every kilometre, and service reminders tell you when the chain, brakes or cables are due — before something starts making noises on the road. Ride history and per-ride effort scoring come as standard.

The Price

Free forever.
No subscriptions.

No accounts. No cloud servers. No ads. Every core feature is free and stays free — there is no trial and nothing is locked behind a paywall.
