Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

NNCNC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application NNCNC (the "Application") and our website nncnc.app.

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Precise Location Data (GPS): To provide our core cycling tracking features (speed, distance, routing, and telemetry), the Application requires access to your device’s precise location services.

  • Background Location: If permitted, the Application may collect your location data in the background (even when the app is closed or not in use) to ensure your continuous cycling route is recorded accurately.
  • Storage: Your route and location data are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your raw GPS coordinates to external servers unless you explicitly choose to export your data.

Device Information: We may collect basic, non-identifiable technical data about your mobile device, including device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for crash reporting and performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the core tracking features of the Application.
  • Calculate cycling analytics such as current/average speed, distance, elevation, and time.
  • Trouhoot technical issues and fix bugs.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or location data to third parties. All route telemetry data remains your personal property and resides safely on your physical device.

We may use trusted third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics for Firebase or Apple Crashlytics) to collect aggregated, anonymous statistics to improve application stability. These services do not receive your precise location trails.

4. Security

We value your trust. We implement standard security measures to protect your local data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@nncnc.app
